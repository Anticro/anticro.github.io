POI Toolbox

POI Toolbox is a map app for adventurers, vacation planners, and active lifestyles, with many useful features for everyday life.

chdv

chdv (Charging Devices) stands for 'Charging Devices'. The app estimates how long a battery needs to charge, creates reminders for the right time, and can also switch smart plugs on and off if you wish.

mtu

mtu (My Time Usage) stands for 'My Time Usage'. With mtu, you can track time quickly and easily. Whether for work or personal use, one tap ends the current activity and immediately starts a new one. Because time keeps moving.
