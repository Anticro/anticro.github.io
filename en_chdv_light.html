chdv

Estimates battery charging times and creates notifications. It also loves turning your smart plugs on and off.

chdv makes it easy to keep track of devices while they charge and estimate when they can be unplugged. The app is easy to use and requires no knowledge of batteries or chargers.

Getting started is easy: Add a 'profile' for a new device and check its battery level once while it is charging. chdv uses this data to predict future battery charging times. More experienced users can also enter the battery capacity and charger power to avoid interruptions and follow-up questions.

chdv creates reminders for charging sessions and, if you like, even HomeKit automations to switch your smart plugs on and off automatically. This lets you use cheaper off-peak electricity while you sleep or power from your solar panels during the day.

Whatever you want to charge, whether it is a phone, power bank, tool, bike computer, toothbrush, or another device, chdv reminds you reliably and is happy to automate your smart plugs.
